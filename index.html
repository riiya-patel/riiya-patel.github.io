Welcome! I am a data analyst pursuing an M.S. in Biomedical and Health Informatics at Case Western Reserve University. I am currently looking for an internship or part-time research opportunity in health informatics, and I am also open to data analytics roles, ideally in the Cleveland area. If your team works in this space, I would welcome the chance to connect.
